Sizing & Fit
All our tees are oversized by design, so they'll fit looser than a regular T-shirt. If you prefer a more fitted look, we'd suggest sizing down. Check the size chart on each product page before ordering.
We recommend going with your usual size for the classic oversized look, or one size down for a slightly more fitted silhouette. Full measurements are listed on every product page.
Shipping & Delivery
We ship free across India. Standard delivery typically takes 7 to 10 days from when your order is placed.
Payment & Cash on Delivery
Yes, COD is available. On COD orders, a small advance payment is collected online at checkout and the remaining balance is paid on delivery. Prefer to pay fully online? You'll get an extra 10% off when you prepay.
No, the advance payment on COD orders is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances, including damages or size issues. This helps us keep COD available while reducing fake or unfulfilled orders.
Returns, Exchanges & Cancellations
Yes. We offer a 7-day size exchange on T-shirts, starting from the date of delivery. A flat exchange fee of ₹80 applies. Reach out to us within 7 days to start an exchange.
We currently do not accept returns or refunds for reasons like a change of mind or design preference. We only offer the 7-day size exchange described above.
Cancellations are only possible before your order has been dispatched. Once it's shipped, we're unable to cancel it. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel.
Product & Care
Our tees are made from 240 GSM heavyweight cotton for a thicker, more premium feel and better drape than standard T-shirts. Machine wash cold, inside out, and avoid ironing directly over the print.
